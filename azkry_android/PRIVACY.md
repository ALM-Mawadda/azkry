# Privacy Policy — أذكاري (Azkry)

**Last updated: 7 August 2026**

أذكاري is an offline Islamic adhkar and prayer-times app. It has no backend, no
account system, and no analytics. This policy describes the one piece of
personal data the app touches and what happens to it.

## Short version

The app collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores everything on your device.
It does not even hold the `INTERNET` permission, so it is technically incapable
of transmitting your data anywhere.

## Location

**What.** If — and only if — you choose "current location" in prayer settings,
the app asks Android for your **approximate** location
(`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`). Precise location is never requested.

**Why.** Prayer times are astronomical: they cannot be calculated without
coordinates. The app computes them on the device from your coordinates and your
chosen calculation method.

**Where it goes.** Nowhere. The coordinates, the resolved city name, and the
time zone are written to the app's private storage on your phone and used only
for that calculation. They are never transmitted, shared, sold, or logged to any
server. The app has no network permission and no server to send them to.

**Your control.** You can decline the permission, revoke it later in Android
Settings, or enter a city manually instead. Declining does not disable the app —
it falls back to the location you set by hand.

## Everything else you create

Your dhikr counters, favourites, worship tracking, Quran bookmarks, khatmah
progress, reminder settings, and prayer preferences live in a local database on
your device. Automatic cloud backup and device-transfer are switched off for
this app, so none of it leaves the phone unless you explicitly export a backup
file yourself through Android's file picker — you choose the destination, and
that file is then yours to manage.

## Notifications

Adhan and adhkar reminders are scheduled locally by your device's alarm clock.
No push service is involved and no notification data leaves the phone.

## Data we collect

None. No identifiers, no advertising ID, no crash reporting, no analytics, no
usage statistics, no contacts, no photos, no files.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes every piece of data it holds. There is nothing
stored elsewhere to request the deletion of.
